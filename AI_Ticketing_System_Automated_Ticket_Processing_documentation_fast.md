## 1. Product Requirements Document (PRD)

### Overview
The AI Ticketing System with Automated Ticket Processing aims to revolutionize IT support operations by leveraging artificial intelligence to automate routine tasks, intelligently classify tickets, and provide initial resolutions. This feature will significantly reduce the manual workload for IT support agents, accelerate ticket resolution times for end-users, and optimize operational costs for IT management. The system will process incoming support requests, understand their intent, and take appropriate automated actions, thereby empowering human agents to focus on more complex and high-value issues.

### Business Goals
- Improve Operational Efficiency by significantly reducing manual effort in ticket processing.
- Reduce Operational Costs by lowering labor costs associated with handling routine support requests.
- Enhance Customer Satisfaction by providing quicker and more consistent resolutions to end-users.
- Empower Support Agents by freeing them to focus on more complex, high-value, or sensitive issues.
- Scale Support Operations to handle a larger volume of requests without a proportional increase in headcount.

## 2. Functional Requirements Document (FRD)

### FR-1: AI-Powered Ticket Ingestion and Classification
**Description:** The system shall automatically ingest incoming support requests from various communication channels (e.g., email, chat, ITSM portal) and utilize AI to classify each ticket by category, urgency, and impact.

### FR-2: Automated Ticket Routing and Assignment
**Description:** Based on the AI's classification, the system shall automatically route tickets to the appropriate support queue or assign them to specific agents or teams, adhering to predefined routing rules.

### FR-3: Automated Knowledge Base-Driven Response and Resolution
**Description:** For identified routine issues, the AI system shall automatically search the integrated knowledge base, suggest relevant articles, and, where appropriate, provide automated responses or initiate self-service resolution workflows to end-users.

### FR-4: Agent Assist and Recommendation Engine
**Description:** For tickets requiring human intervention, the AI system shall provide real-time recommendations to support agents, including relevant knowledge base articles, similar past tickets, and suggested next steps or actions, directly within their ticketing interface.

### FR-5: AI Model Training and Feedback Loop
**Description:** The system shall incorporate a mechanism for continuous improvement of its AI models, allowing agents and administrators to provide feedback on AI classifications, automated actions, and suggested responses, which will be used to retrain and refine the models.
